About this document

This PDF collects every document in the curriculum package into one file: a critical review of the LLM Training Flow Volume 2 study guide (135 topics, 676 interview Q&As) and of the separate Coverage Gaps and Fact-Check document, the missing and future topics for AI engineers and Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs), a revised 16-week FDE track, and 16 real-world projects with templates and offline starter kits.

What is inside

PartContents
1 · Overview and curriculum reviewHeadline findings; the structural review of Vol 2; a stress-test of the existing gap analysis.
2 · Gap register61 verified missing topics (10 P1, 40 P2, 10 P3, 1 Watch), the 13 P1 additions, and six area files with evidence and interview Q&As.
3 · Errata and fact-check14 corrections to Vol 2 and 57 checked claims from the gap document, with sources.
4 · Future topics and revised syllabusThe 2026–2028 horizon scan and a dated 35-event calendar; the 46-topic FDE core and the 16-week track.
5 · Real-world FDE projectsHow every project runs, the rubric and coverage matrix, then 16 engagement briefs.
6 · Engagement templatesTen reusable templates: discovery memo, data readiness, SOW, ADRs, eval plan, threat model and more.
7 · Starter kitsFor each offline kit: run commands, what it contains, the metric-to-criterion map and its limits.

Where to start

  • Curriculum owners: Part 1, then Parts 2 and 3.
  • Instructors: Part 4 (the 16-week track), then the projects overview at the start of Part 5.
  • Students: one brief in Part 5, its templates in Part 6, and its kit in Part 7.

What is not in this PDF

  • Starter-kit source code. The generators, controls, baselines, harnesses and 371 tests (Python 3.11; P13 in TypeScript) are in the repository and the accompanying ZIP. Part 7 reproduces each kit’s README only.
  • Generated data and results. Each kit creates these when you run it.

How to read the links

Currency and limits

  • Facts are verified as of 26 September 2026, and dated facts carry source links. Law and product facts change monthly: re-verify them before each cohort, as described in Part 4.
  • Coverage was checked against the Vol 2 study guide, not the 890-page full book. Volume 1 was not available.
  • Nothing here is legal advice.
